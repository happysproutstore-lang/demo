About Take Charge Fitness

“I never really thought that working out could be so much fun!! I love all the instructors!”

Conveniently located at 1921 N. Charles Seivers Blvd., just west of I-75 from the Clinton-Norris exit. Take Charge Fitness Program is an 18,000 square foot, facility designed to encourage community fitness, wellness and education.

Read more

Exercise/Gym Area: 10,000 square feet of space is dedicated to fitness and exercise. This area houses resistive, aerobic, and sports specific exercise equipment as well as a walking track.

Designated Exercise Rooms: We have rooms specifically designed to conduct our specialty classes including Aerobics (floating floor), Pilates (Mat and Reformer) and Yoga.

Locker Rooms: Separate locker rooms for men and women feature secure lockers with separate shower and changing areas. Showers and toilets are handicapped accessible.

Anna joined Take Charge Fitness Program staff in 2006. Anna has dedicated her entire professional career to the fitness industry and has been passionate about making a positive impact in individuals’ lives through health and wellness for over 30 years. She has worked in different capacities throughout her fitness industry career, which include Nutrition/Wellness coordinator, operations management, group fitness director, group exercise instructor, and personal trainer. In addition to being the Director of Take Charge Fitness Program, Anna is the current Program Champion for Silver Sneakers in East Tennessee. Anna is married to Steve Dark and they have two sons, Cole and Luke.
