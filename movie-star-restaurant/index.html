Our story

About Movie Star Restaurant

At Movie Star Restaurant, we take pride in serving up the freshest buffet, dinners, and catering in town—just the way our momma made it. From our kitchen to your table, where every bite feels like family.

At Movie Star Restaurant, we believe the classics never go out of style. From crispy fried chicken that tastes like home to genuine Southern hospitality, everything we do comes from the heart.

Read more

Our story began with our mother, who first became known for her fried chicken and kind spirit while cooking at a little gas station in Purvis. From there, we moved into the old Movie Star lingerie factory, and even after building a new home, the name stuck—it just felt right.

Today, we carry on her traditions by making everything fresh each day with the best ingredients and welcoming every guest like family. Over the years, many customers have become dear friends, and we’re honored to be ranked among Mississippi’s best buffets year after year.

We're the home of the best fried chicken in Hattiesburg and we believe that all food should be made fresh with love.
