About River Road Go-Kart

Here at River Road Go-Kart Track you can race your friends in a 6 car heat in our 9hp Dino karts. Fear not, there will also be something for the younger kids with our single and double seat karts. We also offer league tournaments.

We are family owned and operated, and here to bring a new and exciting form of family entertainment for all ages for the southeast Louisiana area. We look forward to providing you with great service and plenty of Thrills and Spills, so come out and see us at River Road Go-Kart Track where we like to go fast and you can go fast too!
