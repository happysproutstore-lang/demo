About us

About Animal Boutique

Animal Boutique started more than 25 years ago in Wanda Rollins' garage and has grown over the years to become one of the Augusta, GA, area's most popular and professional grooming and boarding facilities. We offer a clean, comfortable and stress-free environment for your dog or cat whether they are here to stay or just to play. Our kennel techs are Fear Free Certified and have the knowledge and tools to keep your pets happy and healthy while they are in our care.

Our kennel and grooming staff are all pet lovers first and you will find them to be the most caring professionals in the area. We provide quality grooming at a reasonable price. Our skilled groomers are equipped with the best equipment available as well as quality shampoos and conditioners to assure your pet's safety and comfort. Our Fear Free Certified groomers treat your pet with love and respect and we work hard to make sure your pet feels good and looks good when we get done.

Read more

Animal ownership is the ultimate expression of man's ability to love and care for the many creatures that touch our lives and become part of our family as if they were our very own children. As animal lovers and pet care professionals, we recognize that the love of our four-legged friends carries even more responsibility.

We do our best to get your pets in and out in a timely manner. We do a lot of work with dogs who have been traumatized at home or other grooming facilities, and pets that have severe matting or other health issues, so sometimes your pet may be here a little longer than you would like. We always put your pets' needs first so please let us know of any trouble they have had in the past or any health issues they may have. While your pet is here for grooming, we do take them out to go potty and they do have water. You are more than welcome to bring a snack for them while they are here.

We do encourage boarding reservations especially for major holidays. We do have limited space available for our large dogs, so give us a call as soon as you know you will need us!

Are you curious as to where your pet will be staying? You are more than welcome to stop by for a tour of our facility Monday-Saturday 9am-2pm.
