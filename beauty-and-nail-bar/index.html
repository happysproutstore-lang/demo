About Beauty & Nail Bar

Beauty and Nail Bar Salon is a professional beauty platform in Brighton that provides top-notch luxurious beauty treatments to its clientele. We are extremely passionate about what we do and our individuals are dedicated to providing beauty services of the highest quality. Having hands-on experience of many years, our professionals are trained to know how to pamper the clients. Our services are an expression of our identity. We do not believe in using average products and techniques; our business paradigm revolves solely on high quality. Regardless of the package or service our clients opt for, one thing is for sure: they will leave our doors feeling fully satisfied and elated.

Read more

We offer a myriad of cutting-edge beauty services such as lash extensions, premium waxing, eyebrow tinting and shaping, express gel manicure and pedicures, massage treatments and a lot more. All advanced techniques and technologies are used. We provide every service just the way you want it. Everything is fully tailored to your needs; whether it is simple threading or eyelash extensions, our professionals listen to your requirements first before starting the beauty ritual. Client satisfaction means everything to us. We go to every length to ensure that every single client of ours is happy, relax and completely satisfied with our service. Our individuals are extremely soft-spoken and professional. Clients can talk to them and share their concerns or queries regarding anything and they will assist them to the maximum.

Beauty and Nail Bar Salon is known for providing the best facials in town. We make use of the best exfoliants, cleansers, essential oils and moisturizers so that your skin is replenished during the process and you walk out with a 100-watt skin out of our doors. Numerous facial treatments are available. Get in touch with our consultants and they will guide you which facial would be perfect for your skin type and concerns. Our staff is highly trained and certified. They know how to work their magic while our clients sit and relax in our soothing environment. The individuals will effortlessly massage the products in your skin so that every pore is cleaned and your skin is rejuvenated.

The main factor which differentiates us from other salons is our prices. We do not believe in charging extra for anything. Our packages are quite economical as compared to the quality of services are clients get. Beauty and Nail Bar Salon believes that every woman has the right to feel more beautiful and money should not come in the way of luxurious beauty treatments. Therefore, our prices are competitive so that they come in the reach of every client. Moreover, we have an accommodating schedule and we strive hard to make sure that all clients get their services on the allotted slot.

Get in touch with our assistance team anytime you want and book yourself a pampering ritual today. We promise not to disappoint you any of our services. You will definitely come back for me, that is a Beauty and Nail Bar promise!

With a number of beauty salons present in the area, we can proudly say that we have no parallel. Our professionals know exactly what they are doing. What differs us from our competitors is our passion for delivering the best only. ‘Average’ is a word unknown to us. We strive to deliver the best and we fulfill all our promises.
