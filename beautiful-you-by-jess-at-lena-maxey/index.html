About Beautiful You by Jess at Lena Maxey

Your neighborhood board certified Medical Esthetician, Electrologist, Advanced Eyelash Technician and owner/operator of Beautiful You, Jessica Fuller, has over a decade of experience and education focused on advanced skincare, electrolysis, hair removal and spa services. Skin rejuvenation, health and anti-aging are just a few of the benefits of regular treatments with Jessica at Beautiful You. She is dedicated to finding the latest technology and treatments to keep you looking your best and continually advances her education and training to keep pace with the latest industry innovations.

Check out all of the services on the tab above and click the "Make an Appointment" link when you're ready to discover your Beautiful You!

Read more

Beautiful You offers a variety of facials including deep cleansing, acne, multivitamin, teen, oxygenating, Vitamin-C enzyme and a variety of advanced chemical peels. For full details about all skincare services, please click the Advanced Skincare Services link. The latest in Image and Logor skincare products are also available for purchase at Beautiful You.

Temporary and permanent hair removal services are offered at Beautiful You. Jessica is an expert "waxologist" and offers traditional temporary hair removal and shaping using both strip wax and hard wax.

Additionally, Jessica is a board certified electrologist offering permanent hair removal services. This advanced technique is the only FDA approved method to permanently remove all hair types. For full details about all hair removal services, please click the Hair Removal link.

Eyelash extensions provide longer, fuller, very natural looking eyelashes. One lash at a time is applied onto the existing lash. They are water-proof; you can shower, exercise and even sleep with them on! Your own eyelashes will grow out on a continuous cycle, so touching up the lashes will help to maintain the fullness of lush lashes. Re-touching is highly recommended every 2-3 weeks. Click the Eyelash Extensions link for more details.
